# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static single-file HTML/CSS/JS (`index.html`), no build step. Existing codebase; keep it static.

## Users

Two audiences, weighted equally:
- Private drivers from Los Palacios y Villafranca and nearby (Sevilla) with a dent, scrape or collision damage, checking whether the workshop is trustworthy and how to get a quote.
- Customers sent by or dealing with their insurance company, confirming the workshop is serious before handing over the car.

Most arrive on a phone, often from Google Maps or a search.

## Product Purpose

Website of Rubiocar, a body-and-paint workshop (chapa y pintura). Success is contact: a phone call, a WhatsApp message, or a quote request with the form (which opens email or WhatsApp prefilled).

## Positioning

Local workshop, direct treatment with the person who repairs the car, factory colour matching with professional Syrox and Axalta paint systems, works with insurers.

## Operating Context

Six-step repair process: diagnosis and free quote, dent removal, filler and primer, sanding, oven paint (colour matching), polishing and delivery.

## Brand Commitments

Name "Rubiocar". No existing logo asset. Spanish-language site.

## Evidence on Hand

- Real photos of the workshop and some finished jobs exist on the business's Google Maps listing; the user will download the ones uploaded by the business into `fotos/`. Pending at time of writing.
- No testimonials, reviews, ratings, certifications or before/after pairs are on hand. Never fabricate them; AI imagery must not be presented as real jobs.

## Product Principles

- Contact is always one tap away, especially on mobile.
- Show the craft (process, materials, real work) rather than claiming quality.
- Local, direct and honest: no invented numbers or reviews.
